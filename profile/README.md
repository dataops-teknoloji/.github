# DataOps Teknoloji

**From data to decision.**

We build AI infrastructure inside organisations' own systems, and AI agents that do the work end to end for vertical industries. Your data stays in-house; every answer can be traced back to its source.

## Products

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **DataOpsRAG** | On-premise enterprise AI platform. Brings documents, databases and models together in one secure layer inside your own infrastructure, works with open-weight models, and returns every answer with its source. |
| **MizanHub** | AI agents for accounting and tax. Runs the repetitive month-end checks end to end and presents each result with its reasoning. |

## How we build

- **Data stays in-house.** Models run on the organisation's own infrastructure.
- **Every answer is traceable.** No source, no answer.
- **Open by default.** Open-weight models and open standards, no lock-in.
- **People decide.** Agents do the work; a person makes the call.
